# WalkerV2: first walking policy

Goal: a first velocity-tracking walking policy for WalkerV2, with every task parameter derived from the
robot's own physics, not copied from G1 or BHL. Actuator numbers are placeholders until the real2sim
motor characterization lands. They all live in one file and are domain-randomized meanwhile.

## What the physics says (details in walker_v2_first_principles.md)

- **14.8 kg, COM 0.568 m** at the ready pose. LIPM omega = 4.16 rad/s.
- **Gait period 0.72 s** (omega * T_step = 1.5, same as G1). The teammate's walkingV6 video shows ~0.7 s.
- **No ankle roll.** Lateral balance comes only from hip roll and step timing. Hip roll is the most loaded
  joint relative to its job: ~24 Nm when the COM is on the midline at foot switch.
- **Reach-limited speed.** At the 0.659 m ready height the foot reaches ~11 cm ahead/behind, so about 0.6 m/s
  at this cadence. Faster than that, the robot has to crouch. Commands start at vx [-0.4, 0.8] and widen to [-0.5, 1.0].
- **Gains** (MIT-mode kp/kd for the drivers): hip pitch 60/5.5, hip roll 120/7.5, hip yaw 20/0.5, knee 115/3.0,
  ankle 85/1.5. Rule: kp = worst-case quasi-static torque / sag budget, bounded by the armature bandwidth and by
  saturation. The knee budget had to tighten to 0.1 rad because knee sag goes one-to-one into torso pitch:
  at kp 60 the PD-only stand diverged at 0.8x kp.

## Task design

| Piece | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Actor obs (41) | gyro, projected gravity, q - q0, qdot, last action, command (vx, vy, wz), gait clock sin/cos | Everything is measurable on the robot. **No base linear velocity**: the VectorNav can't measure it. The critic gets it as privileged info |
| Critic obs | actor + base lin vel, foot heights, air time, contact, contact forces | Standard asymmetric actor-critic |
| Action | joint targets: q0 + scale * a, scale = 0.25 * peak / kp | An action of 1 asks for 25% of peak torque at zero speed |
| Actuator | explicit PD + linear torque-speed clip (`DcMotorActuatorCfg`), 0-10 ms command delay | Matches MIT mode on the Robstride drive |
| Gait | clock with period 0.72 s, duty 0.55, `gait_contact` reward; both feet down under a stand command | The LIPM gives the rhythm, so the policy doesn't have to find it. One float of state to reproduce on the robot |
| Swing | **`swing_height` (positive)**: the swing foot tracks 0.07 sin(pi s) over its clock swing phase, 0 in stance; plus mjlab's `foot_clearance` / `foot_swing_height` penalties at 0.07 m | 0.07 m is ~10% of leg length. The positive term was added after v1 (see Results) |
| Foot slip | weight -1.0 (mjlab default -0.1) | v1 slid its feet because slipping was nearly free |
| Foot flat | cost on the sole's tilt | No ankle roll: without this, edge-walking pays off in sim and won't transfer |
| Feet too close | linear cost below 0.18 m lateral separation | Stance width is 0.29 m. Crossing the feet is how a hip-roll-only biped falls |
| Posture | mjlab `variable_posture`, tight when standing, hip roll/yaw kept tighter than pitch when walking | |
| Terminations | tilt > 60 deg, base < 0.35 m, 20 s timeout | |

### Domain randomization (the stand-in for real2sim)

| Unknown | Range | Replaced by |
|---|---|---|
| kp / kd as seen at the joint | x0.8-1.2 / x0.7-1.3 | real2sim step-response fit |
| Rotor inertia (armature) | x0.5-2 (log-uniform) around 0.015 / 0.004 / 0.008 | real2sim |
| Joint friction | x0-2 around 0.2 / 0.1 / 0.15 Nm | real2sim |
| Torque limit | x0.8-1.0 of peak | real2sim torque-speed curve |
| Command latency | 0-10 ms | CAN timing measurement |
| Base mass / leg masses | x0.85-1.2 / x0.9-1.1 | weighing the robot |
| Base COM | +/-2.5 cm xy, +/-3 cm z | |
| Foot friction | 0.4-1.2 | |
| Encoder bias | +/-0.015 rad | joint calibration |
| Pushes | every 3-6 s, up to 0.4 m/s | |

**When real2sim lands:** update `MOTORS` and `GAINS` in `src/th_training/robots/walker_v2.py`, narrow the
matching DR ranges in `env_cfg.py`, rerun `walker_v2_first_principles.py --md` and `sim2sim stand`, then retrain.
If the drives turn out not to be linear torque-speed (back-EMF, current loop), swap `DcMotorActuatorCfg` for
mjlab's `BuiltinDcMotorActuatorCfg` or a `LearnedMlpActuatorCfg` trained on the logged data. That's one function, `_actuator_cfgs()`.

## Deploy contract (for th_controllers)

All of it is in the ONNX metadata (`th_training/export.py` writes the real kp/kd; mjlab's own export says kp=1):
joint order, default pose, action scale, kp/kd, effort limit, `policy_dt` 0.02, `gait_period` 0.72.
The obs order is `base_ang_vel, projected_gravity, joint_pos - q0, joint_vel, last_action, command, [sin, cos](2 pi phase)`.
The normalizer is inside the ONNX graph.

## Results

### v1 (`v1_clock`): dead end, stopped at iteration 1000
No falls, but the policy **shuffled**: mean swing apex 1.1 cm (target 7 cm), `gait_contact` stuck at the
"both feet always down" value, reward flat from iteration 500. Cause: every swing term was a *penalty* that
only fires when a foot moves (`foot_clearance` is weighted by foot speed) or lands (`foot_swing_height`).
So never lifting the feet avoided all of them, and foot slip at -0.1 was too cheap to stop sliding.
Fix in v2: a positive clock-driven `swing_height` reward and foot slip at -1.0.

### v2 (`v2_swing`): deployed to `policies/walker_v2/walker_v2_velocity_v2.onnx`
4000 iterations, 58 min on an RTX 5060 Ti (0.8 s/iter). Final training stats: 0 falls from tilt, 4% of episodes
end with the base too low, swing apex 4.5 cm. Iteration 600 only walked forward and turned (it stood still for
backward and sideways commands, a local optimum for small commands). All directions came in by iteration 1500.

### 36 V bus check (2026-11-15)
The motors run at 36 V, not the 48 V the Robstride datasheet numbers are quoted at. Peak torque is
current-limited, so it doesn't change. No-load speed is back-EMF-limited, so it drops to 0.75x
(RS03 20.4 -> 15.3, RS02 42.9 -> 32.2, RS06 50.3 -> 37.7 rad/s). `robots/walker_v2.py` now has
`BUS_VOLTAGE = 36`, which the training actuator model and sim2sim (`--voltage`) both use.

The v2 policy was re-checked with the 36 V curve, and at 30 V (a 10S pack near empty): **no change**. All
tracking numbers match the 48 V run to 3 decimals, so the torque-speed clip never engages. Peak joint speed
as a fraction of the 36 V no-load speed: knee 0.47, ankle 0.22, hip pitch 0.20. That's 0.57 for the knee at 30 V.
No retrain needed. The next training run picks up 36 V automatically.

### Known weaknesses / next iterations
- **Sideways tracking is weak**: about 60% of the command (0.15 for 0.25 m/s). Expected with no ankle roll, but try a larger
  lateral share of commands or a tighter `track_linear_velocity` std.
- **Ankle peaks reach ~70% of RS06 peak (~25 Nm)**, above its 11 Nm continuous rating. Fine for transients. Log RMS
  torque against the rated torque once the thermal model comes out of real2sim.
- The torque-limit DR (0.8-1.0) never bit, since peaks are already under 0.8. The real constraint will be thermal/RMS.
- Hip yaw peaks at 57% of its 17 Nm. RS02 is the smallest motor; confirm the CAD has RS02 there.
- Before hardware: real2sim actuators, IMU pose in `make_mjcf.py`, hip-roll hard stops, a joint sign check
  (CAD_REVIEW.md), and a `th_controllers` node that implements the deploy contract above.
