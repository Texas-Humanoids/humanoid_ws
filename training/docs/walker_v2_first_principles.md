## 5. PD gains
- **kp** = clip(worst-case torque / 0.2 rad, lower, upper). The 0.2 rad is the PD sag the policy has to
  pre-compensate. With action scale ~0.25 rad, that keeps actions around +/-1.
  - lower = armature * (2 pi * 10 Hz)^2 (mjlab's rule), so the rotor's own dynamics are tracked
  - upper = peak / 0.4 rad, so a 0.4 rad tracking error saturates the motor and no more
- **kd** = 2 zeta sqrt(kp I_eff), zeta = 0.5, on the swing-leg inertia I_eff = M_ii + armature, clipped to
  kp * [0.02, 0.1] s. The PD time constant stays within 1-5 policy steps.
- **Knee exception: sag budget 0.1 rad.** With the feet flat, the sagittal sags add straight into torso pitch
  (pitch = -(hip + knee + ankle)). The knee carries the most stance torque, so its sag dominates. Phase 2 (PD-only stand
  in `sim2sim stand`) found that at knee kp 60, the 0.087 rad sag tilts the base back 7 deg and the stand diverges at
  0.8x kp (inside the DR range). With knee kp >= 100 it stands at 0.8x kp, 10 ms delay, tilt <= 7 deg.
- Action scale = 0.25 * peak / kp (mjlab convention): action 1 asks for 25% of peak torque at zero speed.
